Built for people who meet a lot

Everything premium. Nothing to install.

The features you pay for elsewhere, running entirely in your browser.

Live captions, translated on your device

Everyone’s speech becomes captions for the whole room. Pick a language and each caption is translated right in your browser — your words never go to a translation server.

Point, draw & pin

Laser, pen and numbered pins with notes right on a shared screen. The presenter sees them in a floating window over any app.

Verifiable encryption

Peer-to-peer media plus a 6-digit safety code you can read aloud to prove nobody is intercepting the call.

Shared whiteboard

Sketch together in real time, undo your strokes, and save the board as an image.

Highlights that line up

Mark a moment with one key. Highlights land in your notes with meeting and recording timestamps.

Waiting room & host controls

Admit people one by one, lock the room, mute everyone, or remove a participant.

Talk-time balance

A private view for the host of who has spoken how much — measured locally, never sent anywhere.

One-click recording

Everyone’s audio mixed and saved to your device, with a REC badge so nobody is recorded unknowingly.

Auto-Mode meeting director

The stage follows whoever is talking, drawing or pointing. Decisions, deadlines and action items are captured as highlights automatically — and an open mic gets muted if you step away.

Time-travel catch-up

Join late and get a card with what you missed: key moments, recent conversation and the live whiteboard.
